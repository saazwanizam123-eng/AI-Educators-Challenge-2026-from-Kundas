Bahasa Melayu

Asas Suku Kata (KV, KVK, KVKV)

  • KV + KVK: sa-bun (sabun), ru-mah (rumah).
  • KV + KV + KV: ke-re-ta (kereta).
  • Fungsi: Memecahkan perkataan panjang untuk mengurangkan beban membaca.

Sebut setiap warna suku kata secara berasingan dahulu!

Bahasa Melayu

Kata Nama Am vs. Kata Nama Khas

  • Kata Nama Am: Benda umum (huruf kecil) e.g., guru, sekolah, kucing.
  • Kata Nama Khas: Nama khusus (huruf besar) e.g., Cikgu Ali, SK Taman, Si Comel.
  • Peraturan Visual: Kata Nama Khas sentiasa bermula dengan HURUF BESAR.

Contoh: "sekolah" (Am) vs "SK Seri Indah" (Khas).

Bahasa Melayu

Imbuhan Awalan (Prefixes)

  • mem- : mem + baca = membaca.
  • ber- : ber + lari = berlari.
  • men- : men + tulis = menulis.

Imbuhan diletakkan di HADAPAN kata dasar.

Bahasa Melayu

Kata Ganda & Peribahasa Mudah

  • Kata Ganda: Perkataan diulang e.g., rama-rama 🦋, kanak-kanak 👦👧.
  • Ringan tulang: Suka membantu / Rajin bekerja 🧹.
  • Mandi peluh: Bekerja dengan sangat kuat 💦.

Peribahasa mempunyai maksud kiasan tersirat!

English

CVC & Sight Words

  • Short Vowel CVC: c - a - t (cat 🐱), p - e - n (pen 🖊️), d - o - g (dog 🐶).
  • Sight Words: Common words to recognize instantly: the, and, is, it, you.
  • Phonics Tip: Sound out each letter slowly before blending!

Sight words don't always follow standard phonetic rules.

English

Rhyming Word Families

  • -AT Family: cat, hat, mat, bat, rat.
  • -IG Family: pig, dig, big, fig, wig.
  • Rhyme Pattern: Words with matching ending sounds rhyme together!

Change only the first letter to make new rhyming words!

English

Action Verbs (Doing Words)

  • Physical Actions: Running 🏃, Jumping 🦘, Eating 🍎.
  • Sentence Example: "The boy is running fast in the park."
  • Rule: Action verbs describe what a person, animal, or object does.

Ask yourself: "Can I perform or do this action?"

English

Opposites (Antonyms)

  • Size Pair: BIG 🐘 vs. Small 🐭.
  • Temperature Pair: HOT ☀️ vs. Cold ❄️.
  • Feeling Pair: Happy 😊 vs. Sad 😢.

Antonyms are word pairs with completely opposite meanings.

Science

Kitar Hidup (Life Cycles)

  • Peringkat 1 & 2: Telur 🥚 $\rightarrow$ Ulat beluncas (Caterpillar) 🐛.
  • Peringkat 3: Kepompong (Pupa) 🪵.
  • Peringkat 4: Rama-rama Dewasa (Butterfly) 🦋.

Kitar hidup berpusing semula dari dewasa ke telur!

Science

Keadaan Bahan (States of Matter)

  • Pepejal (Solid): Bentuk tetap e.g., ais, batu, kayu 🧊.
  • Cecair (Liquid): Mengikut bentuk bekas e.g., air, susu 🥛.
  • Gas: Memenuhi ruang e.g., udara, stim 💨.

Ais (Pepejal) cair menjadi Air (Cecair) dan mendidih jadi Stim (Gas).

Science

5 Deria Manusia (The 5 Senses)

  • Penglihatan & Pendengaran: Mata 👁️ (Sight) & Telinga 👂 (Hearing).
  • Rasa & Bau: Lidah 👅 (Taste) & Hidung 👃 (Smell).
  • Sentuhan: Kulit / Tangan ✋ (Touch).

Setiap organ deria menghantar isyarat ke otak kita.

Science

Benda Hidup vs. Bukan Hidup

  • Benda Hidup: Bernafas, membesar, makan & minum (Manusia, Haiwan, Pokok).
  • Benda Bukan Hidup: Tidak bernafas atau membesar (Kereta, Batu, Kerusi).
  • Ujian Ringkas: Adakah ia perlukan makanan dan air untuk hidup?

Tumbuhan juga benda hidup kerana ia memerlukan air dan cahaya matahari!

Mathematics

Visual Fractions

  • $$\frac{1}{2}$$ (One Half): 1 out of 2 equal parts 🍕.
  • $$\frac{1}{4}$$ (One Quarter): 1 out of 4 equal parts.
  • $$\frac{3}{4}$$ (Three Quarters): 3 out of 4 equal parts.

Top number (Numerator) = parts taken; Bottom number (Denominator) = total parts.

Mathematics

Money Skills (RM)

  • Banknote Colors: RM1 (Blue 🔵), RM5 (Green 🟢), RM10 (Red 🔴).
  • Coins: 10 sen, 20 sen, 50 sen.
  • Shopping Example: Have RM5 - Buy Nasi Lemak (RM2) = Change **RM3**.

Recognize money by color for fast visual processing!

Mathematics

Telling Time

  • Short Hand: Points to the **Hour** 🕐.
  • Long Hand: Points to the **Minutes**.
  • Example: Short hand on 3, long hand on 12 = **3:00 o'clock**.

When the long hand points at 12, it always means "o'clock" or "00 minutes".

Mathematics

Visual Addition & Subtraction

  • Ten-Frame: Imagine a 10-grid box to count items quickly.
  • Addition (+): Join two groups (e.g., 5 🔴 + 3 🔵 = **8**).
  • Subtraction (-): Take away from a group (e.g., 7 🍎 - 2 🍎 = **5**).

Addition makes total numbers bigger; subtraction makes total numbers smaller.
