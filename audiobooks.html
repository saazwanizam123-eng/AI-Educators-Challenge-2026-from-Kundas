The Story of Sang Kancil and the Giant

Sang Kancil was a small and clever mousedeer who lived happily in the jungle.

One day, a giant came to the jungle and wanted to eat the animals.

Sang Kancil used his clever idea to trick the giant and save his animal friends.

The giant was fooled, and all the animals escaped safely into the forest.

This story teaches us that being clever and wise is more important than being big and strong.
